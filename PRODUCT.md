## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: peers and collaborators.** Engineers and makers who arrive already
holding a reason to be here — from GitHub (`c0ze`), the blog, Mastodon, Bluesky,
or a conversation. Their job is to work out the shape of what Arda builds, find
something worth their attention, and keep a line open.

**Secondary: Arda himself.** The site is home base — the personal index that
points at everything else he makes.

Explicitly *not* optimised for recruiters, hiring managers, or consulting lead
capture (confirmed 2026-07-25). Those visitors are served by
`resume.arda.tr`; this surface owes them nothing extra.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal index for Arda Karaduman — Systems Architect in Tokyo.
It gathers professional work, side projects, games, writing, and music in one
place and routes visitors onward to the site that actually holds each thing.

Success: a peer leaves understanding the breadth of the work, and with somewhere
to go next. A click *away* from the site is a success, not a leak.

## Positioning

The range is the mechanism. A Systems Architect whose actual span runs from
embedded processor design through cloud infrastructure and legacy-to-AI
integration (MCP), *and* who ships consumer apps, browser games, atmospheric
black metal, and songs written in Tolkien's Sindarin.

No neighbouring "developer portfolio" can truthfully copy that combination. The
breadth is not clutter to be managed — it is the differentiating claim
(confirmed 2026-07-25).

## Operating Context

- Single-page experience; sections are About, Projects, Games, Music, Writing,
  Contact.
- One of four sibling sites: `resume.arda.tr` (professional dossier),
  `blog.arda.tr` (writing), `ai.arda.tr` (AI chat), `lind.arda.tr` (Sindarin
  song project). Outbound project links leave the family entirely
  (`sudone.jp`, `vigil.today`, `skriv.ist`, `fable.tr`, `pagan.tr`).
- Inbound traffic is social and referral, not search-intent or ad-driven.
- Some visitors arrive from music channels (Discogs, Metal Archives, Last.fm)
  rather than software ones.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Stack:** Vite 8, ReScript 12 compiled to JS and rendered by React 18,
  Tailwind 3, `next-themes`. npm only, Node 24.
- **Architecture boundary:** page sections and all copy/data live in ReScript
  (`src/components/*.res`, `src/content/*Content.res`); TypeScript is a thin
  interop shell only. Content never hardcoded in components.
- **Generated files:** `src/config/site.generated.ts` from
  `config/site.config.json`; `config/themes.json` is the generated cross-repo
  theme contract; `resume.arda.tr`'s `theme.css` is generated by its own script.
- **Theme system today:** a 9-theme "Ink & Ledger" catalogue, machine-checked
  across all four repos by CI (`theme-contract.yml`), including two AAA
  high-contrast modes (Paper, Carbon).
- **Everything above is in scope for the redesign** — the catalogue, the theme
  count, the cross-repo contract, and the ReScript architecture itself
  (confirmed 2026-07-25).
- **Open decision:** whether a new visual system propagates to the three sibling
  sites, and on what schedule. Not decided; must not be assumed either way.

## Product Principles

1. **Peers, not prospects.** No conversion funnel, no persuasion scaffolding, no
   manufactured urgency. The visitor is already interested; respect that.
2. **The range is the argument.** Breadth must read as one deliberate person,
   not a list of unrelated hobbies.
3. **An index that points outward.** The site's job is to route, credibly and
   quickly. Sending someone away is the win condition.
4. **Content is data.** Copy and project records stay in `*Content.res`, out of
   components, so the site stays editable without touching layout.
5. **Claim only what is real.** The evidence on hand is genuinely strong; it
   never needs padding.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Established commitments that survive any redesign:

- WCAG-conscious contrast in every theme, with at least one AAA-targeted
  high-contrast light mode and one AAA-targeted high-contrast dark mode.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` fully respected — ambient motion freezes and
  scroll-revealed content is shown unconditionally.
- Semantic sectioning and headings; keyboard focus states and hit targets
  preserved.
