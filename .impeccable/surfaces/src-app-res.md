## Scope & mode

`arda.tr` root single-page catalogue. Visitor mode: **Experience** — the work
leads from the first viewport and the interface recedes.

## Audience, job, action

Peers and collaborators arriving from GitHub, Mastodon, Bluesky or the blog;
plus Arda himself using the site as home base. Job: work out the shape of what
he builds and find somewhere to go next. Success is an outbound click, not a
form fill. Explicitly not a recruiter or client funnel.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** One table in which a Kubernetes migration and an atmospheric black
metal record are the same kind of object. Refuses the developer-portfolio
arrangement: hero name, stat trio, project grid, contact CTA.

**OWN-WORLD.** Uncoated cool grey-white stock (`#F2F1ED`) or the same catalogue
on microfiche film (`#0E0F12`). 1px hairline rules carry every structure; zero
border-radius; zero shadow; zero translucency. Archivo Narrow condensed doing
catalogue-header work over B612 Mono — an Airbus flight-deck face — carrying
every numeral. Eight resistor colour-band values encode kind and nothing else.

**STORY.** A peer understands within one viewport that the range is a single
practice rather than scattered hobbies, then leaves through a link.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Masthead rule carrying catalogue no., entry count, kind
count, revision and Tokyo time. Beneath it a three-up featured strip in full
colour — SUDONE, Pagan, A Dark Room — each citing its own catalogue number.
Then a solid black FULL CATALOGUE bar, a seven-band facet row with per-kind
counts, and the ruled table beginning in the same viewport.

**FORM.** Parts distributor catalogue; candidate 7 of 7 on the grounded list;
staged as featured-plus-annex (open-storage annex staging); seed key `679ef8bc`.

## Memorable moment

The black FULL CATALOGUE bar: the featured strip has just shown three wildly
different artifacts at scale, and the bar drops the visitor into a flat table
where all three are simply rows with part numbers. The demotion *is* the
argument.
